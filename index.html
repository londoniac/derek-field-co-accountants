About the Practice

Trusted in East Grinstead. Connected across Europe.

Derek Field & Co is an established ICAEW Chartered Certified firm based on East Grinstead High Street. We combine the personal attention of a local practice with the technical depth to support complex, multi-jurisdictional work.

Our clients range from individuals and sole traders to multi-million pound companies with operations across the UK and Europe. That breadth is deliberate — it keeps our advice grounded, practical, and commercially aware.
